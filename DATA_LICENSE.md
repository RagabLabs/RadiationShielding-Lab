The application source license does **not** grant a license over NIST Standard
Reference Data. Source copyright notices and provenance are retained in the
original responses in `data/raw/`. The normalized JSON is a reformatting/subset
of the cited source tables; coefficients have not been re-evaluated or invented.
Formatting, extraction and edge-side annotations were added on 2026-10-14.

SRD 126's source cover states copyright years 1989, 1990, 1996, with the U.S.
Secretary of Commerce acting on behalf of the United States. The online XCOM
home page and distribution page also reserve copyright; do not infer that SRD
is public domain because NIST is a federal agency or because web access is free.
